THE HISTORY OF

ASTON VILLA

Founding

Aston Villa was founded in 1874 by four members of the Villa Cross Wesleyan Chapel cricket team. Seeking to maintain theirphysical fitness , they gathered under a gas lamp to discuss forming a new team.

It is believed that their first-ever match was against Aston Brook St Mary's Rugby, with the first half played under rugby rules and the second half under football rules.

1874

Golden Era

In the 1890s, Aston Villa had an unprecedented run of league wins, securing 5 out of 7 league titles between 1894 and 1900. No team would be able to replicate this level of dominance until Liverpool in the 1970s, over 80 years later.

During this period, in the 1898/1899 season, Aston Villa also won the English Double, claiming both the top division title and the FA Cup. They became only the second club in English football history to achieve this feat, with no other team accomplishing it again until Tottenham in the 1960s.

Aston Villa's six league titles made them the most successful club in English football until they were surpassed by Arsenal in 1953, when Arsenal won their seventh title.

DOMINANCE

European Royalty

Due to winning the league in 1981, Aston Villa qualified for the European Cup, the premier competition for all league winners of the European leagues.

Aston Villa won the most prestigious club competition in the world, defeating the likes of Dynamo Kyiv (USSR), Anderlecht (Belgium), and Bayern Munich (Germany) in the final.

Aston Villa became the sixth English winner of the European Cup in a row, during a period of English dominance. Between 1975 and 1985, there were 11 finals of the European Cup, and an English team reached 9 of those finals, winning 7.

CHAMPIONS

Modern Era

On November 1st, 2022, Aston Villa appointed esteemed football coach Unai Emery, former PSG and Arsenal head coach.

Despite taking over midway through the season and with the club much closer to the relegation zone, Unai Emery helped Villa finish 7th, qualifying for European competition for the first time since 2011.

In his first full season, Unai Emery led Villa to a 4th-place finish—their highest position in 31 years. He also secured qualification for the Champions League, the highest level of European competition, for the first time in over 40 years.
